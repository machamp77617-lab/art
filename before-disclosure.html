Installation

Before Disclosure

Year
2026
Medium
Photographs, oil paint, found document, tracing paper, Japanese stab binding
Components
2

The first component involves portrait photographs of public figures connected to the Epstein case (Jeffrey Epstein, Donald Trump, Elon Musk, and David A. Ross), over which I hand-paint black squares covering their faces. The black square is the same visual form used in media coverage to protect victims' identities. Here, applied to the powerful, it asks: who does concealment protect?

Hand-painted black square, oil on photograph, gold frame, 2026
Hand-painted black square, oil on photograph, silver frame, 2026

The same gesture that shields survivors from exposure has long operated in reverse, obscuring the faces, names, and networks of those with wealth and institutional access. The black square does not discriminate by intention; it is a form of omission. In painting over these figures, I do not accuse but enact the same erasure the legal and financial system performs on their behalf.

The second component is the 119-page New York Grand Jury DOJ document concerning the Epstein case, every page fully redacted to black by the Department of Justice before public release. I have printed each page on tracing paper and bound the document using Japanese stab binding. The resulting book is legible as object: a structure of held-back language, a volume of authorised silence. Light passes through the pages. The redactions stack and accumulate. Nothing is disclosed.

Hand-painted black square, oil on photograph, white frame, 2026
Grand Jury-NY, 119-page DOJ Epstein document, printed on tracing paper, Japanese stab binding, 2026

Together, the two components examine concealment as a system operating across media, law, and institutional power, and the double silence that protects perpetrators and names victims at once.
